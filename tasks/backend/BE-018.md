# BE-018 — Reservation queue, hold expiry and allocation

## Task information

- Owner: Backend
- Status: Planned
- Priority: P1

## Outcome

Allocate copies through a deterministic reservation queue with durable hold, expiry and cancellation behavior.

## Dependencies

- BE-017
- BE-015

## Interfaces and source of truth

- Consumes: circulation lifecycle from BE-016 and dispatcher protocol from BE-015.
- Produces: core.reservations, reservation allocator and durable reservation event consumers.

## In scope

- Migrate reservations with deterministic queue-order fields.
- Implement create, cancel, claim, hold and expiry allocation behavior.
- Dispatch reservation allocation through the durable outbox protocol.

## Out of scope

- Overdue calculation, email delivery, frontend reservation view and payment rules.

## Test-first sequence

1. Add backend/tests/integration/core/test_reservations.py for deterministic ordering, hold expiry, cancellation and replayed allocation.
2. Run: cd backend; python -m pytest tests/integration/core/test_reservations.py -q. Record the missing allocator failure.
3. Implement reservation persistence, allocator and dispatcher consumer.
4. Run the focused integration test and dispatcher replay test.

## Evidence checkpoint

- Expired hold advances exactly one eligible next reservation.
- Consumer replay cannot skip or allocate one queue entry twice.

## Acceptance criteria

- Queue ordering is deterministic within one copy scope.
- Reservation mutations are tenant-scoped, authorized and audited.
- Hold expiry is durable rather than an in-memory timer.

## Reviewer checklist

- Allocation uses server-side state, not client-provided queue position.
- Cancellation cannot reorder unrelated reservations.
