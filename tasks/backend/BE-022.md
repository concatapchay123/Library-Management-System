# BE-022 — Public members, membership plans and subscription policy

## Task information

- Owner: Backend
- Status: Planned
- Priority: P2

## Outcome

Manage public-library members, configurable plans and subscription validity as a borrowing-policy provider.

## Dependencies

- BE-011
- BE-006

## Interfaces and source of truth

- Consumes: tenant infrastructure from BE-011 and core policy port from BE-016.
- Produces: public-library member, plan and subscription resources plus membership policy adapter.

## In scope

- Migrate members, plans and subscriptions with tenant safety and edition enablement.
- Seed Basic 5 books/30 days and Premium 20 books/90 days as editable organization data.
- Resolve active subscription policy through the core interface.

## Out of scope

- Fines, invoices, payment providers, webhooks and frontend membership screens.

## Test-first sequence

1. Add backend/tests/integration/public_library/test_membership.py for subscription validity, plan selection and tenant isolation.
2. Run: cd backend; python -m pytest tests/integration/public_library/test_membership.py -q. Record the missing membership-policy failure.
3. Implement migrations, services, policy adapter, contracts and API adapters.
4. Run the focused integration test and core loan policy test.

## Evidence checkpoint

- An inactive subscription cannot provide a borrowing policy.
- Plan defaults are tenant-scoped seed data, not controller constants.

## Acceptance criteria

- Core circulation remains independent from subscription persistence.
- Member and subscription relationships use composite tenant constraints and RLS.
- Edition-disabled organizations cannot access public-library APIs.

## Reviewer checklist

- No money state is introduced in membership policy code.
- Plan changes do not mutate a loan policy snapshot.
